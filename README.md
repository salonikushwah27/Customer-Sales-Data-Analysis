## 📊 Key Insights

**Overview**
- The business generated **222M** in total revenue from **9K customers**.
- The average purchase amount is **24.65K**, the average customer age is **43.42**, and the average feedback score is **5.14** out of 10.

**Revenue by City**
- **Kolkata leads with 55M**, about 25% of total revenue.
- The other five cities (Delhi, Bangalore, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Chennai) are close together at **33M–34M** each.
- Kolkata earns roughly 60% more than any other city, which makes it the strongest market.

**Monthly Revenue Trend**
- Monthly revenue is stable, ranging from **17.0M to 20.6M**.
- **October is the peak month at 20.6M**, likely due to festive-season demand.
- **September and December are the lowest at 17.0M each.**

**Top 10 Customers**
- The top 10 customers contribute about **1.36M combined, which is under 1% of total revenue**.
- The largest single customer (Jennifer Smith) is at 210K.
- Revenue is spread widely across the customer base, so there is **no dependence on a few buyers**.

**Customer Demographics**
- **55.16% of customers are male (4.96K) and 44.84% are female (4.03K).**

**Customer Feedback**
- **Feedback score 2 has 1,724 customers**, about twice the count of any other score (779–838).
- This spike in low ratings needs a review of the causes, such as product quality, delivery, or service.

**Customer Growth by Year**
- Customer count grew from **408 in 2020 to 1,749 in 2021**.
- It then stayed steady at about **1,750–1,865 per year** from 2021 to 2024.
- 2025 dropped to **1,412**. This should be checked, since it may be a partial year of data or genuine churn.

## ✅ Recommendations
1. Study what works in Kolkata and replicate it in the other cities.
2. Run targeted campaigns in September and December to lift the low months.
3. Investigate the feedback score 2 spike and fix the root cause.
4. Launch loyalty or retention offers to reverse the 2025 decline.
